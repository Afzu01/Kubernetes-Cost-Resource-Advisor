Useful Product Preview

Kubernetes Cost & Resource Advisor

A lightweight advisor for small teams that identifies overprovisioned Kubernetes workloads, recommends safer CPU and memory requests, and estimates monthly savings before changes are deployed.

Who It Helps

  • Startups trying to lower cloud spend without hurting reliability
  • Platform teams reviewing request and limit waste across services
  • Student and open-source teams running on small infrastructure budgets
  • Engineering managers who want a simple before-and-after optimization view

Impact Snapshot

Before vs After Allocation

Illustrative cost model based on monthly CPU and memory reservation pricing.

Actionable Output

Workload Recommendations

Each card shows observed usage, safer recommendations, estimated monthly savings, and rollout risk.

Why This Matters

Built to Help Real Teams

Cloud Cost Reduction

Shows teams where they are paying for reserved capacity they rarely use.

Safer Right-Sizing

Uses P95 plus a safety buffer instead of aggressive average-based downsizing.

Operational Clarity

Gives engineers a readable report before touching deployment manifests or autoscaling rules.

How It Works

Prototype Flow

01

Observe

Collect CPU and memory telemetry from workloads over time.

02

Estimate

Use P95 usage as a safer resource envelope than raw averages.

03

Buffer

Add conservative headroom to reduce throttling and out-of-memory risk.

04

Recommend

Return right-sized requests and a cost impact view for adoption.
